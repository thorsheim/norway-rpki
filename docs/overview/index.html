Networks with full ROA coverage and ASPA

Routed networks where every announcement is RPKI-valid and the AS publishes an ASPA, so their routes are covered by route-origin validation and, where ASPA is enforced, path validation. That is not a clean bill of health: a few still have a high-severity finding, a wide maxLength that lets a forged-origin more-specific validate, and others have smaller loose ends. They are listed under Remaining findings; the country pages explain each one. Largest IPv4 space first; click a column to sort.
